## Label policy

`VERIFIED` is reserved for wording or an artifact directly observed in the cited source. `UNVERIFIED` means the requested signal or current status was not independently established. `BLOCKED` means access failed. `INFERENCE` marks our synthesis of multiple observations, while `RECOMMENDATION` marks a product implication rather than a team commitment. A portal or documentation statement is therefore not presented as a personal founder statement unless the source explicitly supports that attribution.
